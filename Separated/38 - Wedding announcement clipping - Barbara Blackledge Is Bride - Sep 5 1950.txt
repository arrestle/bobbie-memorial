SEP 5 1950

Barbara Blackledge Is Bride

X-Ray Technician Wed To Frank J. Restle,
Stanford University Graduate Student

Teaneck — Miss Barbara June Blackledge, daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Frank A. Blackledge of 756 Cedar Lane, became the bride of Frank Joseph Restle Jr., at a four o'clock ceremony on Sunday in the Teaneck Community Church. The bridegroom is the son of Mr. and Mrs. Frank J. Restle of 563 Cumberland Avenue. A reception followed at the home of the bride.

The Rev. W. Carman Trembath officiated at the marriage. Nelson M. Doescher was organist, and soloist was Clemens Quardt.

Given in marriage by her father, the bride wore a ballerina length gown of Chantilly lace with a mandarin collar and short sleeves. Her fingertip veil fell from a headpiece of Brussels lace and she carried white roses and pompons.

Miss Joan Retta Blackledge was her sister's only attendant. She wire [sic — "wore"] a lime green ankle-length dress with a taffeta sash and a matching picture hat. Her flowers were white pompons and talisman roses.

William DeWitt Wall of Easton, Pa., a college classmate of the bridegroom, was best man. Ushers were Lloyd Richard Blackledge, brother of the bride, Edward G. Hoch, Jr., brother-in-law of the bride, both of Teaneck, and Edwin Scull of Reading, Pa.

The bride is a graduate of Teaneck High School and Ridgewood Secretarial School, attended Columbia University and trained as an X-Ray technician at Santa Barbara (Calif.) Hospital. She was employed as X-Ray technician at St. Joseph's Hospital in Paterson.

The bridegroom, also a Teaneck High School graduate, received his B.A. Degree from Lafayette College in June, and was elected to Phi Beta Kappa, national honorary fraternity. While serving in the U.S. Army, he attended Yale University for one year, and was stationed in Japan for two years.

Mr. and Mrs. Restle are travelling to Palo Alto, Calif., where Mr. Restle has been accepted as graduate student at Stanford University.
