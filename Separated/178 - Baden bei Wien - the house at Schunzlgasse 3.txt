Family recollection (Andrea): Schunzlgasse 3, Baden bei Wien, Austria, the first house the Blackledge family stayed in there.
The front of the house from the street, with a curved gable and a round window. A man and three children stand on the pavement by the railings, and someone looks out of a first-floor window.
People not named.
Scan 87 is a second scan of the same four prints; the Scan 84 copies were kept (the two scans are equally sharp).
Family recollection (Andrea): Bobbie is the youngest in all these photos. The family lived in Egypt (1928) before moving to Austria, so these date from about 1929-1930 (estimated), when Bobbie was 3 or 4.
