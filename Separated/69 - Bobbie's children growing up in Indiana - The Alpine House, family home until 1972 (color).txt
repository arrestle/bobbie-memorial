The Alpine House, on Fox Hollow Road north of Bloomington, the family home until it was sold in 1972 (family recollection, Andrea). Same house as #84 and #87.
A printed date stamp on the top border is cut off at the edge of the scan and could not be read reliably (it may end in "62").
The Alpine House was listed for sale by Properties Unlimited (listing #148, $60,000, possession June 15, 1972; PhillipScans/Copy of photo09282026001218_004.jpg). Family recollection (Andrea): it was sold in 1972, and the family lived in two temporary houses while the Dittemore Road house was built, moving in about 1974.
