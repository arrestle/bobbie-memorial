# Bobbie with the plane, spring 2017

The only photo we have of Mom with her plane, a Cessna Skyhawk (N687SP). On the back, in her handwriting:

> Spring 2017
>
> Taking the new IU Media School Dean James Shanahan on plane flight to see eagle nest on our 900 ac Bottom Rd refuge. He is one with sun glasses. His grandfather in the 1900's donated the family farm in OH to the first land trust in the state just as we did. I then had the pilot fly over Lk Monroe with a watershed in 6 counties. The sediment rate is alarming & can be seen from the air. Floods are extensive this spring. I wish I was able to still pilot a Cessna. [Tom?] Great Pilot - we will fly again!

Copied word for word, with her spelling and abbreviations kept. The word in [brackets] is hard to read; it may be the pilot's name.

She was 90. That February she had given $100,000 to the IU Media School to found the Barbara Restle Press Law Project.

Her story "Born to Fly" (in this folder) tells of an earlier flight over Lake Monroe, in the 1960s, when she flew the Cessna herself.

Also in this folder: the chain gang photo, the children as child labor on the farm wagon, 1964 (#159).
