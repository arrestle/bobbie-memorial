Family recollection (Andrea): the children helped drain the swamp to turn it into cornland. The photo shows clay drainage tile being laid behind a tile-laying machine on the Bottom Road field along Bean Blossom Creek, the same land later restored to wetland and donated to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service as the Restle Unit (family recollection, Andrea).
The 1992 Herald-Times article (#49) says the Restles drained their Bean Blossom Creek bottomland on Bottom Road with tiles and pumps, and that the Fish and Wildlife Service tore that tile out about 1990 to restore the wetland before she donated it. See environmentalism/timeline.md, reference [39].
