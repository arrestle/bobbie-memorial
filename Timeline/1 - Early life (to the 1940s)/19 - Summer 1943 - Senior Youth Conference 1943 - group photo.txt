Printed on the photo (Scan 59): "SENIOR YOUTH CONFERENCE - 1943 -". Scanned sideways; turned upright.
The back (Scan 60, #138) is signed by about 20 people, including Barbara B. Blackledge.

Research (September 2026):
- "Senior" means the high-school age group; church summer conferences were split into junior and senior sessions. Bobbie was 17, a Teaneck High School senior.
- Signers who wrote "Uncle" and "Aunt" (Uncle Mort, Uncle Duke, Aunt Margaret Babinsky) were adult leaders, as was customary at church conferences.
- Teaneck Community Church was organized under the Classis of Bergen of the Reformed Church in America; its pastor in 1943 was Rev. W. Carman Trembath, who later married Bobbie and Frank (1950).
- Signer Leslie B. Hegeman (born Brooklyn 1927, lifelong church member) fits a Reformed Church youth conference for New Jersey and New York churches.
- The exact site was not found. The Reformed Church's Warwick, NY camp was not bought until 1959. The RCA Archives in New Brunswick, NJ (records to about 1950) would confirm it.
