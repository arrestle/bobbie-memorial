Handwritten caption (album page, Scan 27):
"Mascatuck Wildlife Refuge
US Fish/Wildlife
81ac donated by Restle Family
Bottom Rd. Bloomington IN 1991"

Notes:
- "Mascatuck" is the album's spelling of Muscatatuck (Muscatatuck National Wildlife Refuge). The donated land became the refuge's Restle Unit.
- The acreage and year differ from other sources: the refuge's 1991 annual report gives 78 acres, given in 1990; other sources give 86 or 87 acres. See environmentalism/timeline.md.
