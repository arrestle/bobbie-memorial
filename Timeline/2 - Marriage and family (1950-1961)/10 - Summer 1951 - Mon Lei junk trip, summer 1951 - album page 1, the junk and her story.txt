Album page reassembled from Scans 68 and 69 (the page was larger than the scanner). Levelled by about 1.7 degrees.
The left edge and top-left corner of the page were not captured in either scan, so the start of each line of the handwritten history is missing. A rescan of that edge would complete it.

Handwritten history on the page (words cut off at the left edge shown as [...]):
"[T]rip on Junk 'Mon Lei' [sum]mer 1951 on Long [Isl]and Sound. [Ow]ned by Mr Benett of [Eng]lewood N.J. It belonged [to] Ripley ['Believe it or Not'] [un]til his death. The [shi]p was built in Fuchow [Chi]na before the 2nd World [War]. Probably sometime [arou]nd 1940 she was [sail]ed across the Pacific [to] California, only to be impounded by the US - because of illegal entry. Ripley who obtained this ship from the government, fitted her out elegantly in Chinese fashion and installed a diesel to give this 50' vessel a speed of circa 7 knots under power."
Also on the page: a painted card of the junk (signed at lower right) and three photos of the Mon Lei under sail, one of her with guests on deck.
