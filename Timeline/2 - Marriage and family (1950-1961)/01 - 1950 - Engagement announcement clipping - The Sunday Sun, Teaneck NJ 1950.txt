[Photo] MISS BARBARA BLACKLEDGE

Miss Barbara Blackledge
To Marry Frank J. Restle
At September 3rd Wedding

Mr. and Mrs. Frank A. Blackledge of 756 Cedar Lane, Teaneck, announce the engagement of their daughter, Barbara, to Frank J. Restle, Jr., son of Mr. and Mrs. Frank J. Restle of 563 Cumberland Avenue. The announcement was made recently at a tea and reception for [some] friends and neighbors in the garden of the Blackledge home.

Miss Blackledge, a graduate of Teaneck High School and Ridgewood Secretarial School, attended Columbia University and took X-ray technician training at Santa Barbara General Hospital in California. She is serving as X-ray technician at St. Joseph's Hospital in Paterson.

Mr. Restle, also a Teaneck High School alumnus, was graduated with a B.A. degree, Summa Cum Laude, from Lafayette College in June. He was elected to Phi Beta Kappa, National Honorary Fraternity. As a member of the U.S. Army, he studied at Yale University and served for two years in Japan.

After their marriage on September 3, the couple will travel to Palo Alto, California, where Mr. Restle has been accepted as a graduate student at Stanford University. He will work for a Ph.D. degree in psychology.
