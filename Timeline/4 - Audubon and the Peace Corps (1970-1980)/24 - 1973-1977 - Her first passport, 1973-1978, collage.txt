Collage of Bobbie's first passport (#199-#203), from Scans 94-98: the identity page and the stamped pages 7-13 (page 6, which is blank, is left out).
Family recollection (Andrea): her first passport; it chronicles her journeys.

Identity page: BARBARA JUNE RESTLE. Born June 7, 1926, France. Height 5 ft 5 in, hair auburn, eyes blue. Issued June 7, 1973 (her 47th birthday; Chicago passport agency); expires June 6, 1978. Stamped "CANCELED - NEW APPLICATION 8/8/78".

Her journeys, in date order, from the stamps and her handwritten notes (her notes in quotation marks; [?] marks a hard-to-read date or word):

Summer 1973 - Portugal and Madeira with Kathleen
- 22 June 1973 [or 25]: enters Portugal at Porto. Her note: "Summer w Kathy / MADERA 1973 / Portugal 1973".
- 16 July 1973: back through U.S. Immigration.

Autumn 1977 - across Europe and behind the Iron Curtain
- 9 Sept. 1977: France. Her note: "Sept 9 1977 Paris [with Frank?]".
- 13 Sept. 1977: arrives in England at Folkestone.
- 20 Sept. 1977: leaves England (triangular exit stamp; port hard to read).
- 21 [Sept.?] 1977: Nennig, West German customs on the Luxembourg border (the month could be read as 08).
- About 25 Sept. 1977: enters Hungary at Hegyeshalom, from Austria. Hungarian visa valid to 28 Sept. 1977. Her note: "Budapest".
- 28 Sept. 1977: leaves Hungary at Zahony, on the Soviet border.
- 9 Oct. 1977: Polish border stamp; Polish transit visa, handwritten "11 pazdziernika 1977" (11 October 1977).
- 11 Oct. 1977: East Germany, entering at Frankfurt (Oder) from Poland and leaving at Drewitz into West Berlin. Her notes: "Germany 1977 Frankfurt", "Germany".
- 12 Oct. 1977: back into East Germany at Drewitz, leaving West Berlin.
- 14 Oct. 1977: arrives in England at Harwich; the same day, leaves from Heathrow (her note: "London") and arrives at Athens airport.
- 2 Nov. 1977: leaves Athens; arrives at Madrid-Barajas the same day.
- 6 Dec. 1977: arrives in England at Folkestone.
- 8 Dec. 1977: leaves England at Harwich; arrives at Hook of Holland the same day.
- 2[?] Dec. 1977: exit stamp, place hard to read.
- 20 Dec. 1977: home, through U.S. Immigration, New York.

That autumn followed her 1977 BA from Indiana University.
